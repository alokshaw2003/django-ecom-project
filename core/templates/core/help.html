Help & Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, and click the "Checkout" button on the cart page. Fill in your details, and you're all set!

What are the shipping options?

Currently, we offer standard shipping on all orders. This is a project, so shipping is notional.

How can I track my order?

Once an order is placed, you receive an order ID. For this project, there is no real-time tracking, but in a real-world application, this is where tracking information would be provided.
